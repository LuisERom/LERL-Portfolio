Vienvo Button Deck

The Vienvo Buttons is a row of controls that lets you manage individual cables when the Vienvo Hub is placed in a hard-to-reach spot. By connecting through Bluetooth, this makes it easy to access your cables without having to move the hub, perfect for keeping your setup neat and hassle-free.

Features:

  • CR2032 coin cell battery included.
